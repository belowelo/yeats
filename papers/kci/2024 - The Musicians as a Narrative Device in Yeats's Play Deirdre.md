## Abstract

The ancient Greek chorus in drama is often seen as an ancient form of a narrative device. Yeats's play Deirdre uses the Musicians/ chorus slightly differently from the way Greek plays do: Yeats's technique of telling a story through the musicians de-familiarizes the classical device in multiple ways. In Deirdre, the narrative device is designed to make the audience form preconceptions about characters and to affect the drama's action.
