## Abstract

Though Yeats was a prolific playwright throughout his life, describing hisliterary achievements in terms of the dramatic, it cannot be said that Yeats has hadhigh reputation as a playwright. His plays have been underestimated by moderncritics. In defense of the value of his plays, the paper analyzes and refutes critics'arguments.
