## Abstract

Yeats asserts himself as a last Romantic, but we need to rethink Yeats's assertion. This article tries to prove that many studies regard Yeats only as a romantic. Yeats may seem to have a Romantic temper, but, in reality, he does not stick to it. Yeats tries to hide his spontaneous feeling and depends on other objects. One of them is "mediumship." Therefore, he should be called an imagist and a symbolist. Eliot and Pound also regard Yeats as an imagist and a symbolic poet. This paper intends to prove Yeats's Romantic express techniques are intended to adopt these trends of Imagism and Symbolism.
