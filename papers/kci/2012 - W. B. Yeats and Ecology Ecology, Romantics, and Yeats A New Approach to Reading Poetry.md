## Abstract

This article discusses Yeats's ecological poems. Probably this is a rare article in Yeats scholarship, which treats him as an eco-poet. In fact, eco-poetry is not new poetry, as we see some scholars deal with traditional poets, such as Shakespeare. My essay is also an attempt to give eco-critics some correction in the direction, because they are following something like Marxist and Feminist criticism. Poetry is not a political slogan, nor is it a useless thing.
