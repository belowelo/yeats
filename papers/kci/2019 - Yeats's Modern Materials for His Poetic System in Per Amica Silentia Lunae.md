## Abstract

In Per Amica Silentia Lunae, Yeats describes his materials which come to be a foundation for his poetic system of the world and men. These materials and the relationship between the world and men can be a new way of looking at the twenty-first century. In the fast-changing world of present time, modern critics or scholars agree to such interconnected relations of our world and suggest new ways of thinking and different perspectives. My paper will discuss their perspectives and Yeats's use of materials, seeking modernity in particular in the materials.
