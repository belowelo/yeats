## Abstract

William Butler Yeats uses Celtic mythology as materials for his early poems and gives an impetus to the period of the literary Renaissance in Ireland. The Celtic mythology acts on the medium for connecting the ancient Irish territory to the present one, and enlightens the Irish on the importance of history, culture, national identity, and the independence of Ireland. The mythical figures such as King Goll, King Fergus, Cuchulain become the archetype of typical human beings for the Irish. Therefore, Yeats hoped that the ancient Celtic culture could be restored to the new literary Renaissance in Ireland and would make a new turning point in the studies of ancient history.
