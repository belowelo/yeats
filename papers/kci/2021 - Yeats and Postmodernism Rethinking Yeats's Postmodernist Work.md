## Abstract

This is a short survey review of books on Postmodernism, which may be of some help to reading Yeats and his "postmodern" work. What is Modernism or PostModernism, by the way? Could you define it in a word? Maybe not. I also am in difficulty when someone asks me the same question, and that's why I look into several books with the specific question in mind. And I would finally like to suggest that we use the periodic divisions of the literary movement in history, instead of using -isms, such as Romanticism, Modernism, Postmodernism.
