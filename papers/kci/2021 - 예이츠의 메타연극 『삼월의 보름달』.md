## Abstract

This essay discusses the meta-theatrical features in Yeats's play A Full Moon in March. The play is analyzed on the basis of Hornby's five possible varieties of conscious metadrama, so that Yeats's meta-theatrical devices can clearly be demonstrated. As a result, this essay aims to offer a possibility of defining Yeats's plays as metatheater.
