## Abstract

The works of Yeats and Sri Aurobindo remained perennial theme, source and structure for poetry in later phases of their lives; Yeats's last phase has made him, evolved his poetic greatness and Sri Aurobindo to write his great epic and transformation of soul, journey of soul until it merges with Supermind. Yeats's A Vision is geometrical symbolism and phases of Sun and Moon in twenty eight phase of Principles and faculties of human and civilization, while Sri Aurobindo's Life Divine is about spiritual evolution, efforts of superhuman kind towards super mental consciousness by two ways one to lift mind through matter, to Overmind and then to Supermind, sat-chit-ananda. The philosophical book 'A Vision' by W. B. Yeats is a system, the myths served for his poetry to score excellent poetic achievement, Sri Aurobindo's Life Divine based on eternal and spiritual exploration, labyrinth in achieving ultimate reality. Life is continuous, breath is necessary for life to grow when man is dead the body organism are no more usable for life, life withdraws when bodily functions cannot function.
