## Abstract

Swedenborg's mystical thought is not an element that stimulates human emotions, but approaches the knowledge and realistic sense that separates the boundary between facts and truth. This gave Yeats knowledge and inspiration by providing a point of contact and a fixed point between fact and fantasy, the spirit world and reality, and religion and science. Swedenborg says that the whole universe is already in spiritual balance and unity because the Lord rules. On the other hand, Yeats considers it the duty of poets to find the power and driving force of balance that maintains the world while embracing them equally.
