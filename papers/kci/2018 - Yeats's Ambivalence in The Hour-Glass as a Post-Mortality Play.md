## Abstract

W. B. Yeats represents both the Wise Man and the Fool, the conflict between the Wise man and an Angel, and the opposition between the Wise Man and his pupils. Through the play more emphasis is laid on the Fool: the Fool's life is more important than the Wise Man's life. Especially, it is characteristic of post-mortality play. The play shows the Wise Man's internal conflicts, when dying, and describes the transmigration of his soul, suggesting how his soul will be in the afterlife. This play opens up the realm of post-mortality play by surpassing the structure of Western mortality plays such as Oedipus the King.
