## Abstract

William Butler Yeats was interested in astrology in his life, making its structures and critical factors set up his system of thought. The Cat and the Moon exposes the astrological elements in the behavior of both Blind Beggar and Lame Beggar. Blind Beggar is a dark part of the crescent moon, Lame Beggar a bright part of the crescent moon. Blind Beggar with Lame Beggar on his back symbolizes a crescent moon. Also, the moon changed in a cat's pupils represents the astrological factors: its energy influences a cat's life. Like this, Yeats sings us the phases of the moon in the play, which have an effect on the conflict and harmony between self and anti-self, body and soul as well as human fate and human personality.
