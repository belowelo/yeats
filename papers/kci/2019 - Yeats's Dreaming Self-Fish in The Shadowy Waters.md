## Abstract

How is Yeats's ownership of the transient moments dreaming in his poetic drama, The Shadowy Waters? This essay is to look into his yohoba transitional part of dreaming entry and exit: how successfully or skillfully Yeats displays the tricky in-coherent mystic quality, based on the concept of ownership, agent, and location; whether his poetic creativity for the neutral properties of hallucinogenic dreaming is flexible and natural; finally, are we satisfied with the denouement and the climax of the chain of events on the inebriated ship?
