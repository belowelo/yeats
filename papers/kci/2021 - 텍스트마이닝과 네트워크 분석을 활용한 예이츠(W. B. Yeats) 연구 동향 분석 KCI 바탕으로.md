## Abstract

This study was to identify the research trend on W. B. Yeats and provide information necessary to establish the research direction on him. In August 2021, 910 journal papers were collected with the keyword "Yeats" from KCI. Final 854 titles of the journal papers were selected as targeted data and analyzed through Excel, TEXTOM, and UCINET/NetDraw program. Through this analysis, the publication status of Yeats-related researches by year and journal, main keywords of Yeats-related researches, and degree centrality between major keywords were investigated.
