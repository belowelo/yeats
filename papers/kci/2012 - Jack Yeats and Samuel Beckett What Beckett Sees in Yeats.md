## Abstract

Jack Yeats is the younger brother of W. B. Yeats. Jack is known as a painter, but not well known as a writer. But interestingly, Jack as writer seems to have given Samuel Beckett some influence; in the meantime, Beckett paid attention to Jack as writer and artist. This article introduces Beckett's critiques on Jack's art, analyzes Jack's style of art and literary works, and also discusses and compares the plays by the two writers. Beckett's play shows some influence he seems to have received from Jack Yeats.
