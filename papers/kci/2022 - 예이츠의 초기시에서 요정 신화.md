## Abstract

This paper analyses W. B. Yeats's poems on fairies, who has inspired modern poets with his fertile imagination, and looks into the origins of the unity of culture which he pursued. Fairy mythology was a life, a history, and a religion of the Irish people. Fairies were memories for the ancient Irish people, spirits and personifications of their ancestors, and epiphanies of gods and heroes. This fairy mythology is the root of Irish culture. At this root begins the principle of life, sophistication, tradition, the Otherworld, and ethnic superiority. Fairy mythology acts as a mediator between the mind, religion, history, philosophy, and the culture of the ancient world and those of the modern world. Therefore, by accomplishing the unity of culture with Irish fairy mythology, Yeats was a pioneer who signaled us to know the importance of culture in Ireland and all over the world.
