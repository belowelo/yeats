## Abstract

Much influenced by Nietzsche's philosophical thoughts, Yeats's aristocracy focuses on the role of elite figures with a heroic spirit in Ireland's chaotic situation. Yeats promotes an individual's inner autonomy from the bondage of political ideology and materialism. If his aristocracy is considered as a value system that can elevate an individual's inner self, rather than a hierarchy determined by property or bloodline, it can be the basis of gaining more human freedom and diversity. Instead of being prepared as an artificial artistic utopia, it is intended to provide the aristocratic realm in real life and to share the values ​​of art and science among all members of the community.
