## Abstract

This paper aims to explain the characteristics of the concept of nature and civilization in some poems of W. B. Yeats and Ted Hughes. These two poets, though they lived in different times, seem to stand in the same tradition of nature poetry. Yeats gradually opened and amplified the use of nature in consideration of civilization: these two cannot be separated. Hughes, in the tradition of nature poetry, seems to follow Yeats. But he shows his own characteristics through the description of the force and energy of nature. And his nature in his poems has strong connection with human civilization and then reveals the human condition in this world. Hughes reveals the relations of nature and civilization as his own poetic inspiration.
