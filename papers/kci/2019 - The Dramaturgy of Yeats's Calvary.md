## Abstract

Yeats's Calvary is a unique Passion play with an interesting use of chorus.
Yeats resorts to the traditional way of employing the chorus to a certain extent, but the meanings of the songs and the action in it are more intricately connected than in traditional drama. The imagery of the birds depicted in the songs offers another dimension to the interpretation of the subjectivity and objectivity of the play's world.
