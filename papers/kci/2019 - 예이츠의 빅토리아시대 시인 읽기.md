## Abstract

This research tries to read Yeats's theories of literary criticism in his prose works to figure out what his criteria of creative works are in three of his contemporary Victorian poets: Tennyson, Browning, and Arnold. Yeats believes good poets should be able to provide vision and these Victorian poets are incomplete or unsatisfactory. So Yeats decides to create his own criteria, a new literary tradition or modern poetics.
