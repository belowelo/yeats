## Abstract

William Butler Yeats (1865-1939)'s sixth volume Responsibilities has 31 poems and was issued in 1914, in his age of 49. Many Yeats scholars thought Responsibilities as a crucial dividing point in Yeats's poetic world. It is between the fifth section The Green Helmet and other Poems and the seventh section The Wild Swans at Cool and simultaneously and naturally evolves from The Green Helmet and other Poems into The Wild Swans at Coole in the poetic structure. Especially Yeats focused on the poet's responsibilities in the past, present and future. John Unterecker classified the poems as five areas, like these, supernatural responsibilities, social responsibilities, personal responsibilities and aesthetic responsibilities. And he also classified the poems according to the responsibilities respectively.
