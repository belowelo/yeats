## Abstract

Yeats's poetic aesthetic is dynamic, progressive vertical, Yeats sort reconciliation by aspire and descend. The metaphysical elevation is emergent from reality, whatever may mythical, mystical or earthly, Yeats raised the subject matter, his poetic argument, spiritual significance as union. He fused eternal meaning wherein all things are harmoniously unified and culturally and spiritually significant in the ancientness. Yeats associated journey, by water, conflagration as a form of purification to chisel soul element from earthly life in perfect form either manifested in art or visionary eternal world. His later phase poetry turned eternal and artistic significance of abstractism of his self.
