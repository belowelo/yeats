## Abstract

This paper aims to confirm how much influence Nietzsche has had on Yeats. We can see that Yeats has much in common with Nietzsche: ideas such as the praise of heroes, wars and conflicts, the prediction of the end of the western material civilization and praising the brutal and terrible beauty, his harsh criticisms of Christianity, the pride of nobleman, and also his praise for the tragic joy. Also they shared the view of seeing history as something cyclical, the belief in Dionysian life which promotes passion over reason, and the desire for the arrival of some heroic figure.
