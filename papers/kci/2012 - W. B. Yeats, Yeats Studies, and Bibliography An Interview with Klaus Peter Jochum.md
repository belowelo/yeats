## Abstract

Klaus Peter Jochum, one of the greatest bibliographers of Yeats studies, talks about Yeats and Yeats studies. His efforts are still ongoing to put together the vast amount of writing on Yeats and what still expands on Yeats the poet and the man. It is a great honor for the editor to have an interview with him and hear his enlightening views of Yeats and Yeats studies. I hope the readers will enjoy what follows and benefit by his wisdom.
