## Abstract

The love story between Aoife and young Irish warrior in "The Grey Rock" has been received in terms of Yeats's attitudes towards politics and arts. Also alluded in the story is a frustrating love story between Yeats and Maud Gonne. Aoife's harsh criticism against the young man who chose "false faces" instead of true love reflects Yeats's mind towards his living goddess, Maud Gonne, who left the poet for "the poor lout." Aoife's grief was healed by the wine made by Goban. The wine symbolizes arts. That is, what Yeats wants to communicates is that arts is the only way to get him out of his troubled past.
