## Abstract

This paper examines how Yeats uses myth as a creative force to unify reality and imagination, the art and the life, and the natural and the supernatural. Unlike other poets, Yeats heavily uses local and Irish legends and tales, Greek and Roman myths and philosophers, and biblical and other religious myths throughout his poetry. He deliberately refers these various myths to infuse the art and the life for higher truth in literature.
