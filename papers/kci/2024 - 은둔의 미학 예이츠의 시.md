## Abstract

This paper searches for the value of seclusion in W. B. Yeats's poetry. Standing at the crossroad, Yeats had to make his life reorganized in order to focus on writing poems and leading his marriage life. Seclusion consists of place, time, and hermits, so it can be analyzed into the divided sections respectively. The seclusion leads to the productivity to him in creative poems as well as in his personal life. That is, during this period he could gain the precious family, at the same time, his later poems. Furthermore, the tower can be the expanded symbol of Ireland haunted by the heartbreaking history. This paper would analyze the related poems: "Meditations in Time of Civil War," "Coole Park, 1929," "Meru," "Lapis Lazuli," "The Tower."
