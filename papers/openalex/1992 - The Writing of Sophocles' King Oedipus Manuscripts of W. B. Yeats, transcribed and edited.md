## Abstract

No Yeats scholar has done more than David R. Clark to develop our appreciation of the poet's versions of Sophocles' two Oedipus plays, so it is fitting that he should now edit the manuscripts which have for so long been the object of his scrutiny as a critic. Needless to say the result is a model of patient and astute scholarship and one cannot but hope that the American Philosophical Society will be enterprising enough to encourage the editors to tackle Oedipus at Colonus now. Completing translations of the two plays marked a high point in Yeats's career and they hold many keys to a full understanding of his own artistry and thinking in the final decade of his life.
