## Abstract

As a modernist reaction to bourgeois ideology which perceive the given world as the only explainable reality, W.B. Yeats and T.S. Eliot both search for a spiritual reality, the Absolute, beyond this material world. This research aims to inquire how their yearning for a spiritual illumination connects the poetry of Yeats and Eliot with Platonism which makes a separation between the material world and a world of Forms or Ideas. Yeats and Eliot try to reach the Absolute in their own particular ways, and their spiritual efforts lead them to look beyond the limitations of time, space and ego. This paper thus analyzes the Platonic resonances in Yeats's and Eliot's handling of time, space and ego.
