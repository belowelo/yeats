## Abstract

The poem of Ode a Grecian Urn written by John Keats and the poem Sailing to Byzantium written by William Butler Yeats were created in different times,but they still have so many similarities,especially in the aspects of writing skills and ideological content.And they all expressed the same theme,which the two poets pursue the reason and the perfect eternity.
