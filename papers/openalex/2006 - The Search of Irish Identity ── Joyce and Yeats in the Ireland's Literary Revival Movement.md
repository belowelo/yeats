## Abstract

In the Ireland's literary revival movement, Yeats tried to search Irishness from Celtic culture and reconstruct the Irish identity. He and his works played an important role in the movement. Being different from Yeats' idealized depiction of rural Ireland and the past, Joyce tried his best to reveal the psychological state of modern Dublin citizen and the reality of Ireland's subject-matter. He chose a way of silence, exile and cunning and got a more real understanding of Irish identity from a deep sense of cultural inferiority.
