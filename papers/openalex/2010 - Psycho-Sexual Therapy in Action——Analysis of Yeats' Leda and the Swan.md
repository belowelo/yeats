## Abstract

W.B.Yeats' poem Leda and the Swan is a true reflection of the poet's life.The paper analyzes and interprets the poem by considering the supplementary biographical information of W.B.Yeats.The paradox and ambiguity are really the rough love experience and the conflicts between body and soul.Through getting dream and reality together and integrating sex with imagination,the poet's own love gets distilled and achieves a psycho-sexual therapy in action.
