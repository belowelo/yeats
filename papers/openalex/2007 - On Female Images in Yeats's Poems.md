## Abstract

In the study of Yeats, the famous Irish poet and playwright, at home and abroad, people tend to focus much on Yeats' passion and love of Maud Gonne.a beautiful Irish actress.Most of them believe that in Yeats' poems, Yeats eulogizes her beauty and his own sadness because of loss of love. By classifying and summarizing numerous female images in Yeats' poems, we can know that there is versatility of female images in Yeats' poems,as well as richness in expression.
