## Abstract

A non-believer of Christianity,W. B. Yeats instead believes in various occultisms and mysticisms from the East and West. Nonetheless he is not prevented from using Christian culture in his literary creative writing. This paper looks into Yeats' poetic,fictional and dramatic works for the biblical and Christian cultural elements alluded,drawing the conclusion that Yeats regards Christianity and other beliefs equally as his mythological materials.
