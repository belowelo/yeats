Includes full texts and commentary on the following poems: The Song of the Happy Shepherd The Lake Isle of Innisfree The Sorrow of Love When You Are Old Who Goes with Fergus? To Ireland in the Coming Times The Hosting of the Sidhe The Moods The Lover Tells of the Rose in His Heart The Song of Wandering Aengus The Lover Mourns for the Loss of Love A Poet to His Beloved To His Heart Bidding It Have No Fear The Valley of the Black Pig The Secret Rose He Wishes for the Cloths of Heaven Adam's Curse No Second Troy The Fascination of What's Difficult September 1913 Paudeen Fallen Majesty The Cold Heaven The Wild Swans at Coole In Memory of Major Robert Gregory The Fisherman Broken Dreams Ego Dominus Tuus Sailing to Byzantium The Tower Meditations in Time of Civil War Nineteen Hundred and Nineteen Leda and the Swan Among School Children.
