## Abstract

William Butler Yeats is the great Irish poet in the late 19 th and early 20 th century famous for his keen interest and practice in mysticism. Through studying Yeats' s biography and poems associated with Goethe's famous work Faust, the author of this thesis has not only found that there're many similarities between Yeats and Faust, but also drawn the conclusion that Yeats is the inheritor of the spirit of Faust. Therefore, whether Faust of the Middle Ages who takes the devil's power, or Yeats of the modern time who is keen on mysticism, essentially they both have the forever pursuit of art and knowledge, as well as the deep love for truth, goodness, and beauty.
