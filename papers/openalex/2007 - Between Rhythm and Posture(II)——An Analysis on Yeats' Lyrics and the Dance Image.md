## Abstract

In Yeats' Poems,in contrast to his contemporaries,the vehicle of Dance is mainly female.With a study of Yeats' thought on gender,this paper arrives to a conclusion:the female dancer in Yeats' Poems is subjective;dance among the whole nation——Dance image as Primordial Image:with inserting Dance image into Irish local myths and legends background,Yeats awakes Collective Unconscious of Irish which contains national trait,stimulates the joy and sorrow Psyche remains of Irish,pursues and identifies the national identity.
