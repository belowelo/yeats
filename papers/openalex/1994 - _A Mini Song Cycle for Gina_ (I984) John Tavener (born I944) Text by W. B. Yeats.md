## Abstract

Abstract Tavener's highly individual cycle may appear simple at first sight, but a polished performance will require considerable artistry and concentration from the interpreter. The rapt atmosphere must be sustained through the almost continuous vocal line of this extended scena. The simple word-setting and natural rhythmic flow have a folk-like quality; the basically straightforward idiom is devoid of vocal gimmickry, but full of surprises.
