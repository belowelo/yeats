## Abstract

Yeats wrote many poems in metaphysical light. This paper investigates the winding image which often appears in Yeats′ poems and interprets its implications related to the poet′s mystical philosophy. Furthermore, a comparative study is made between Yeats′ mystical philosophy and Chinese Yin yang and Five Elements theory. It discovers the similarities as well as differences in mode of thinking between them. To be aware of these similarities and differences might be very helpful to Yeats′ readers within Chinese cultural and philosophical context.
