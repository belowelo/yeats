## Abstract

Yeats,the most outstanding poet of the late symbolism movement,promotes the poetry aesthetics to a high degree by consciously using artistic skills,and explore the symbolism theory.Through the analysis of his poetics,we found that his poetry writing hides a complex relationship between the internal and external world,which demonstrates his unique concepts of poetics.
