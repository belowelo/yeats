## Abstract

The ultimate "impersonal" is the non-human spirit communicator – or "author" – who dictates the words, during a séance with a medium or session with a ouija board, to the passive human author-receptor who writes them down. What is going on in this literary process? Why was it undertaken in the beginning, how does it evolve, what is the outcome? What is its effect on our judgement of the results?
