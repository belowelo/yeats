## Abstract

Taking its cue from Duffy's edited anthology, Answering Back. Living poets reply to the poetry of the past (2008), this chapter examines how Clarke, Duffy, Lochhead and Meehan have generated dialogue with iconic national poets of the past. It places these conversations in the context of feminist revisionism, and refers to work by Eavan Boland, Sinead Morrissey, Adrienne Rich, and Dorothy Wordsworth amongst others. It recognises that in addition to their feminist enterprise, the poets today salute, as well as adjust, the nation-building potential of their forebears. The chapter is organised under three subheadings, Wordsworth, Burns, Yeats and considers work which Clarke, Duffy, Lochhead and Meehan wrote prior to their laureate appointments as well as poetry and cultural activity undertaken during and as a result of their tenures.
