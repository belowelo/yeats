## Abstract

The copyright of this extravaganza is held by Amnesty Education Limited; all royalties from the book's sales will go to Amnesty International, a worldwide grassroots movement that promotes and defends human rights. But despite this very serious purpose, the book itself is fun. The mystery, although involving the bizarre deaths of numerous cops on the case, really concerns the holograph manuscript of a supposed final novel by James Joyce. Entitled Y8S=+! (Yeats Is Dead!), it consists of six hundred pages of numbers and signs, pure conceptual literature . . . released from the narrow cage of meaning. Abstract art in literary form. A new literature for the new world.
