## Abstract

The play of Yeats'The Death of Cuchulain mainly composed of three scenes and five Fragments.Through such a dramatic structure,Yeats expressed the popular feelings of Irish nationality,also reflected the develop trends which literature?from a focus on external,objective,rational shifting attention to the inner,subjective and irrational in the end of the nineteen century and the beginning of the twenty century.
