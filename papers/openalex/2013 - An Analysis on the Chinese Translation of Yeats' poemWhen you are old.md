## Abstract

When you are oldis one of the early works of William Butler Yeats, the greatest poet of Ireland, in which he expressed the everlasting love for his lifetime lover Maud Gonne. Being well-known for its simple structure, plain language, this poem has attracted the attention of many translators. This article mainly compares and analyzes four Chinese translations of this poem composed by FU Hao, YUAN Ke-jia, FEI Bai, and BING Xin in terms of form and meaning, and comes into a conclusion that FU Hao's translation is the best then comes YUAN Ke-jia's and then BING Xin's and FEI Bai's.
