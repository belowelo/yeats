What I have to offer then is a rather begrudging piece of astonished admiration for Roy Foster's arts as a biographer. First among these arts is the collection of first-rate, not secondhand, information."Do I have anything new?" the biographer asks in terror. "Am I missing something that is known by another, or, God help me, many others?" Roy Foster delved in some pretty thoroughly excavated archives; so how did he come up with so many fresh finds?
