## Abstract

In his late years,Yeats wrote two poems based on the theme of Byzantium——Sailing to Byzantium and Byzantium,expressing his yearning for a purely spiritual and artistic world.By analyzing and comparing the two poems,this paper discusses the change of themes through the ,symbols,and the poet's philosophical thinking used in the two poems,concluding that from the former poem to the latter one there's a shift of focus on death and eternity,and that the poet's philosophical thinking on death and eternity has become more mature and profound
