## Abstract

It is customary for critics to select The Tower of 1928 as the volume which contains the great poems of Yeats's maturity. The volume consists of thirty poems, some of which had been previously published, in Seven Poems and a Fragment (1922), The Cat and the Moon (1924) and October Blast (1927). In The Tower, as the volume is presented in Collected Poems (1933), Fragments, probably written in 1931, is added, and The Hero, the Girl and the Fool is replaced by The Fool by the Roadside.
