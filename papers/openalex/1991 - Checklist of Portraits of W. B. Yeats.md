## Abstract

This checklist of portraits, which supersedes a preliminary version in YA 3 (1985), describes 129 portraits or sketches, thirty-seven caricature drawings, and thirty-eight studio portrait photographs. Only works made during Yeats's lifetime are included. Dimensions are height × width. The colour of pencil or ink is black unless otherwise specified.
