## Abstract

Yeats has often been labeled as a supernaturalist by critics,but the body issue he examines in his middle and late poems has not received enough attention.This essay intends to trace Yeats' development of this issue in his poetic writings,and attempts to uncover the reason for his changing attitude toward the flesh by considering his personal experience and by putting his poems against a broader historical background.It will concentrate on his exploration of bodily desire,bodily decrepitude,and the ideal of the unity of body and soul,in the hope of revealing the largely neglected aspect of this mysterious poet.
