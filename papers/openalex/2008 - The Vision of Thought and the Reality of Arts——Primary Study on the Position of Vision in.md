## Abstract

A Vision is a very important prose work written by Yeats.However,its value to his poems hasn't been recognized.On the base of tracing its origin and of interpreting its connotation,this article tries to reveal influences upon Yeats' poetic creation in many aspects,and whereby gives a affirmative position to its active function.
