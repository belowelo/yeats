## Abstract

William Butler Yeats was indisputably one of the most significant modern poets. The Lake Isle of Innisfree is one of his most representative works of all his early poems and it crystallized Yeats' sense of nostalgia and homesick. The poem presents initial conceptions of modernism, and this paper intends to analyze the initial features of modernism in this poem. The main idea embodied in this poem and the poetic techniques employed by the poet both illustrate the initial attempt of modernism revealed by William Butler Yeats.
