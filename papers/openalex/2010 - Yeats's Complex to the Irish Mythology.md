## Abstract

William Butler Yeats is one of the greatest poets in 20 century.His poetry is characterized by the strong feeling for his nation and distinct Irish factors.The romantic ancient Irish mythology is the main source of Yeats s imagination.The application of Irish mythology in his poems can help the Irish people restore their collective memory, re - establish the Irish history,and reassure the Irish identity.
