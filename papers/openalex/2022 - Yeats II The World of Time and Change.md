## Abstract

Included in The Wanderings of Oisín and Other Poems (1889) was Time and the Witch Vivien , a symbolic dramatic poem adapted by Yeats from the Arthurian tale of Merlin and Vivien, available to him in Tennyson's Idylls of the King . It is a shortened version of a play Yeats had written between the ages of seventeen and nineteen. 1
