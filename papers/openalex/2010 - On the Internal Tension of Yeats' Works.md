## Abstract

Tension,an important term in neo-criticism,refers to the dialectical relations of contradiction and peaceful co-existence in poetic organism.It is assumed that William Butler Yeats' works exhibit the pair features of private vs.public,private vs.concrete,and local vs.international.Internal tension makes his works polysemous,compatible,dramatic and ironic,thus surpassing the opposition and isolation exhibited by Romanticism.
