## Abstract

Mystical thinking is the source of creation for Yeats all his life.From his collecting Irish folk legend at his early stage to the development into the formation of a mystical system in the later period,Yeats constructed a complete system of his own on mythology.From a post-colonial perspective,the present paper sorts out the process of Yeats' mysticism and its development,being somewhat helpful for the interpretation of the poet's ways and purpose in creation with mysticism,and for a better understanding of Yeats' mystical thinking in his poems.
