## Abstract

Abstract In the preface to this volume, The Oxford Handbook of W. B. Yeats, the editors posed Yeats's question, via Plato's ghost, 'What then?' This chapter offers 'Twelve Speculative Takes' in answer to the question 'What now?' How do we read Yeats in light of the preoccupations of contemporary poetry? How do we vivify Yeats in the environmental turn; the revolt against form; the twenty-first-century media; the wake of MeToo; the nationalism that co-opts his work; the twenty-first-century academy; litigation culture; the institutionalization of creativity; a pervasive climate of buzzwords and advertising? As an epilogue to the Handbook, these speculative takes prompt us to ask, 'What next?'
