## Abstract

Yeats and Auden are great English poets,but because of their different backgrounds of life and writing,there is a great gap in the themes,writing materials and techniques of their poems.This paper tries to analyze them based on several poems to reveal their different poetic voices: one tends to escape from reality while the other is closely involved in it.
