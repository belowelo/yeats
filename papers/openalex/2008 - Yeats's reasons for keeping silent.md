## Abstract

In the year 1915 on being asked by Henry James to write some verses for The Book of the Homeless , a war charity volume edited by Edith Wharton, Yeats wrote a poem stating that he would not write a war poem. It was a collection of basically patriotic poems and prose, including illustrations and music scores, contributed by numerous famous writers and artists, the aim of which was to raise funds for the Children of Flanders Rescue Committee which Wharton founded in Paris. ……
