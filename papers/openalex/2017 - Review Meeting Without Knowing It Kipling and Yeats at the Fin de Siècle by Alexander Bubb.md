New York: Oxford University Press, 2016. Pp. xvi + 272. $99. Ambreen Hai Ambreen Hai Smith College Ambreen Hai, Professor of English Literature & Language at Smith College, is the author of Making Words Matter: The Agency of Colonial and Postcolonial Literature (2009). Her most recent essays include "Motherhood and Domestic Servitude in Transnational Women's Fiction: Thrity Umrigar's The Space Between Us and Mona Simpson's My Hollywood," in Contemporary Literature (2016), and "'There's Always the Other Side, Always': Black Servants' Laughter, Knowledge, and Power in Jean Rhys' Wide Sargasso Sea," in Modernism/Modernity (2015). In progress is a book project tentatively titled "Postcolonial Servitude: Domestic Servants in Contemporary Transnational and South Asian English Fiction."
