## Abstract

By subverting the traditional Western narration of the mythical subject matter of Leda and the Swan,Yeats,in the poem of the same name,examines the impact of Britain's colonial rule on Ireland.The poem,by means of symbolization,suggests that Britain's colonial rule brought about the destruction of social economy,morality and human life in Ireland;and suggests that whether such a rule was a gospel for the Irish national culture and its growth remains largely an open question.
