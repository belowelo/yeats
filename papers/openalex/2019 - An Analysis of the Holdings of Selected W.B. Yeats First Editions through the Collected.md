## Abstract

This paper provides a detailed analysis of the holdings of W.B. Yeats first editions through the Collected Works of 1908 in the Rare Book Collection at the University of North Carolina. In this paper I will first offer a literary and biographical context for this remarkable collection. The central section of this paper provides a detailed descriptive bibliography of the first editions through 1908 currently held by the Rare Book Collection. I will include the following information: title, publication, pagination, illustrations, provenance, binding, and other notes as needed. In the concluding section, I will offer an analysis of the RBC holdings and suggest titles for future acquisition.
