## Abstract

The paper dwells on idiostyle of William Butler Yeats, a renowned Anglo-Irish poet, famous for his interest in esoteric doctrines. We analyse the influence of several esoteric motifs on the stylistic device of metaphor employed by the poet. Besides, we focus on the specificity of the metaphorical contexts in the Russian translations of Yeats' verse
