## Abstract

By using the doctrine of the second coming of Christ and the Archetype of pseudo-Christ in the Bible, Yeats's representative poem Second Coming explains that the coming time wasn't a peaceful and tranquil time,but a disorder time of pseudo-Christ's arriving,the poem was a prophet poem from the Christianity's eschatology.
