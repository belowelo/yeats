## Abstract

It is almost futile to search for copies of Olivia Shakespear's novels (and as hopeless to try to persuade reprint houses to reissue them). Searching for the remnants of her Yeats collection is not quite so difficult. After she died, on 3 October 1938, Ezra Pound came to London to clear up her effects, Dorothy Pound being unwell. Before he left Italy, he wrote to George Yeats on 9 October.1 Pound recalled that "a quarter of a century ago some bloke stole a lot of his [i.e. Yeats's] first editions.
