## Abstract

Alchemy refers to a medieval quest for a kimia (old Persian word for 'elixir') capable of turning copper and other base metals to gold. The poems of W B Yeats set for study in the HSC carry at their heart a series of opposites that defy simplistic reconciliation or moral judgement. Despite the fact that they still 'speak' to us of our own struggle to balance futility and meaning, vitality and age, passion and indifference, the poems are first and foremost an illumination of the heart of a poet who always seemed to reach for more than he was granted. This discussion will offer one way into Yeats' poems, finding an aesthetic interstice that lies between the suffering that marks human existence and our potential for beauty and transcendence.
