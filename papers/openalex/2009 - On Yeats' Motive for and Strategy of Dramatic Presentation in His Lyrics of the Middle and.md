## Abstract

Option for impersonal poetry and its moral function constitute the major changes in Yeats' poetics since the turn of the 20th century,motivating him for a dramatic presentation.Insisting on the cognitive and pathetic subjectivity of the poet,dramatic presentation enables Yeats to mediate the presence of the poet through Mask,a text-based anti-self,which presents the poet's feelings and observations to the reader,encouraging the reader to respond to the moral issues under discussion.Dramatic presentation stands as a balanced resolution to propositions like impersonality and poetry as personal utterances,poetry as a moralizing agent,and the reception of the reader.
