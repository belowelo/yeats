## Abstract

This article proposes a reassessment of Conor Cruise O'Brien's responses to the famous question posed by William Butler Yeats in his poem "Man and the Echo", through which O'Brien explored the degree to which art precipitated political action in revolutionary Ireland. By tracing the evolution of his responses through his career, the article argues that the Northern Irish Troubles did not cause a radical transformation in the way O'Brien answered the question.
