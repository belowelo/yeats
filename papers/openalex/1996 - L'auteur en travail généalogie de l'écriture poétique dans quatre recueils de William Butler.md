## Abstract

The aim of this thesis is to show that evolution of yeats's writing is modelled on transformations of his own lineage, as exemplified by four volumes of poetry which run from 1914 to 1928. In responsibilities (1914), yeats begins by asking forgiveness from his ancestors for having no child, then attempts to fill up that void by creating a now poetic technique which he will bequeath to his literary continuators. In wilds swans at coole (1919), death of robert gregory, who was heir to an illustrious protestant ascendancy family, leads yeats to devise the mask, an artistic theory whose purpose is to translate theme of political power into poetic terms. In michael robartes and dancer, (1921), yeats takes up same theme, with a view to pointing out symbolical links between poet and poem on one hand, and father and daughter on other hand eventually, in tower (1928), yeats suberts christian views. About begetting and trinity. Hence advent of a positive mother figure in this last volume.
