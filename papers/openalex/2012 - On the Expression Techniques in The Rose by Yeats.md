## Abstract

The Rose is a early collection of Yeats' poetry written with various expression techniques,which are characterized with fu(similar to an ode,a descriptive prose interspersed with verse),philosophical style and natural and plain words.These techniques,usually mixed together,not only make his poems capricious,but also give readers insights into his changeable styles in his later creation of poems.
