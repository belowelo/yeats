## Abstract

"Bird" is one of the most common metaphors whether in Chinese or foreign poetry, which is used to expound the abstract concepts of love, freedom, immortality and so on. There are 90 poems contain "bird" in The Book of Songs, and many of Yeats' works include "bird". Therefore, the pages following would take Yeats poetry and The Book of Songs as the research object, combined with the detailed textual reading, to explore the similarities and differences of the metaphorical meanings of "bird" in Chinese and foreign poetry. The results show that although their metaphorical meanings are all related to freedom and love, they are still diverse in some aspects
