## Abstract

Abstract Yeats and Tagore: Tagore went West while Yeats came East. The East for Yeats is one solid unified Occident. That was his orientalism. Tagore's Bengal itself was divided by Curzon (1905). Tagore's Gora is half-Western, half-Indian. Tagore and Yeats are among their nations' first decolonized poets. The Gaelic revival and theatre had very little impact on Ireland. But it influenced West African, Bengali, and Hindi twentieth-century drama. The Bauls influenced Tagore's bhakti; his god is a woman. This essay analyses 'Wild Swans at Coole' and Tagore's 'Balaka' poems. Nostalgia and freedom can be seen in artists who fought for the country's freedom, namely Yeats, Tagore, and Verdi.
