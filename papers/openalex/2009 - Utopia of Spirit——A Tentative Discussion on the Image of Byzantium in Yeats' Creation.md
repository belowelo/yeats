## Abstract

The image of Byzantium in Yeats' creation is a social collective imagination of Ireland in the process of decolonization and,further,it merged so much thoughts and feelings about Yeats' personal experience.Revealing the molded process of image,we can see the arduous course of self construction of Ireland and Yeats marching to mature as a spokesman of Ireland.At the turn of century,the image of Byzantium in yeats' creation deeply reflected the conflict of spirit and internal anxiety of Ireland.
