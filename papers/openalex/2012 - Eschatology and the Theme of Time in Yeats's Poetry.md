## Abstract

Mysticism and symbolism are the top concerns in most current research of Yeats's poetry.It is noted that Yeats's poetry conveys the profound theme of time.Time is both linear indicating the image of decline and cyclic suggesting the image of eternity.Yeats's special views of history circulation and ways available to defy time can be explored from the perspective of eschatology.
