## Abstract

It is a pair of contradictory concepts on the personality and the impersonality in poetry.However,in his poetic creation,Yeats accomplished the transition from personality to impersonality.By way of casting those two elements,he fulfilled the modernized transformation.The use of national subjects,the practice of mask theory,the integration of symbolic systems,the esteeming of traditional artistic forms by Yeats play a key role in the modernized transformation of poetry.
