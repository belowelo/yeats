## Abstract

English Poetry, one of the subject selective courses for English majors in most of China's higher education institutions, is taught for many years in the traditional way. The paper will investigate the possibility in combining the ways and rules of form poetics and cultural poetics in teaching of English Poetry from the perspective of cognitive poetics in the case of William Butler Yeats. The author also puts forward suggestions on such aspects as subject orientation, course arrangement and class teaching and so on.
