## Abstract

Yeats lived in the period of Irish national liberation movement turbulent,his creation is effected by the atmosphere of this era.In order to establish the cultural identity of Ireland,Yeats to the British as the standard for the post-colonial anti-writing.In this anti-writing,by portraying Ireland′s natural environment and people′s living condition,Yeats reflected the writer′s consciousness of homeland and the views of ideal way of life;on the revival efforts of ancient pantheistic religion,works includes the criticism to British instrumental rationality and mechanical logic;while the emphasis system of creation and to Irish culture constitution,these were coincide to the ideas of contemporary ecological holism and cultural ecological.In the Irish cultural identity construction,Yeats′Creation inadvertently contains profound thought of ecological ethics.
