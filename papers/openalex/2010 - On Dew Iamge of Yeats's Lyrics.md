## Abstract

Through analyzing the dew images of W.B.Yeats's lyrics,the dew image which also signifies the love and beauty,is a variant of rose image.Moreover,dew image is juxtaposed with other images to present beautiful mood of poetry.Meanwhile,the absence of dew image foregrounds the alteration of Yeats's style.
