## Abstract

This paper deals with the representation of women in the poetry of William Butler Yeats. The
paper tries to demonstrate ways in which W.B. Yeats conveyed women; woman as Ireland, the
woman he loves (Maud Gonne), woman through the symbol of the rose, women in general etc.
The paper mostly focuses on the poems in which Yeats's expresses his love towards Maud
Gonne who was his everlasting inspiration.
